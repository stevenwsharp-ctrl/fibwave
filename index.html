Room lights

Fibwave sends colours to WLED light strips and Philips Hue lights on your home Wi-Fi. The lights follow the scene, the beat, the drops and the mood of the sound.

WLED strips

Philips Hue

First press the round button on top of the Hue Bridge, then click Connect within 30 seconds. The Hue app shows the bridge IP in Settings → Bridges.

Show Fibwave on your TV

Your laptop sends its screen to the TV. The music also plays on the TV, and Fibwave still hears it. When the TV shows Fibwave, click TV mode.

LG TV

Mac
  1. Connect the Mac and the TV to the same Wi-Fi network.
  2. Click Control Center in the menu bar, then click Screen Mirroring.
  3. Select your LG TV. (Most LG TVs from 2018 and newer have AirPlay.)
  4. Click TV mode below.
Windows
  1. Connect the PC and the TV to the same Wi-Fi network.
  2. On the TV, open the Screen Share app.
  3. On the PC, press the Windows key + K.
  4. Select your LG TV, then click TV mode below.

Xbox

Windows
  1. On the Xbox, install the free Wireless Display app from the Microsoft Store, then open it.
  2. On the PC, press the Windows key + K.
  3. Select your Xbox, then click TV mode below.
Mac
  1. The Xbox does not receive AirPlay by itself. Install AirServer Xbox Edition (a paid app) on the Xbox, then use Screen Mirroring on the Mac.
  2. An HDMI cable from the Mac to the TV is easier and has no delay.

Chromecast or Google TV

  1. In Chrome, open the menu (⋮), then select Cast, save, and share → Cast.
  2. Select Sources → Cast tab, then select your TV.
  3. Click TV mode below.

If Chrome shows your TV or Xbox with “Available for specific video sites”, that device cannot show a Chrome tab. Use an HDMI cable, AirPlay, or a Chromecast or Google TV device connected to the TV. LG TVs from before 2018 do not have AirPlay.

HDMI cable

  1. Connect the cable from the laptop to the TV.
  2. If the TV shows a second desktop, click Full screen. Fibwave asks which screen to use. Select the TV.
